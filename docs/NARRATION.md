# Narration script: Sounding walkthrough (about 4:30)

Read this on camera. Each block matches one section of the screen recording, and the video is re-timed to your take,
so you don't need to hit exact times.

**Recording tips**
- **Camera:** face a window or a lamp, use a plain background, and put the camera at eye level. Look into the lens, not at the screen.
- **Takes:** record in one go, or one block at a time, pausing for about 2 seconds between blocks. If you stumble, pause and
  say that sentence again from the start; I'll keep the clean take.
- **Pace:** speak a little slower than feels natural, as if you're showing a colleague your screen.

---

### 1 · Title card
Hi, I'm Hamza. This is Sounding, my version of Fathom: an AI notetaker that turns a meeting recording into a transcript, notes, action items and clips.

### 2 · Opening the app
The live link opens straight into a demo workspace, with no sign-up and no login.

### 3 · My calls
This is the meetings list. It's seeded with real recorded meetings, and everything you see here was generated by the pipeline: the titles, the meeting types, the one-line summaries, who spoke and how many action items are still open.

### 4 · The 8-person meeting, speakers
I started with the hardest case: an hour-long meeting with eight people. My first speaker model could only tell four voices apart, so I tested it against human labels and added a second pass that clusters voices. It now separates seven speakers, with far fewer mix-ups.

### 5 · Playback and transcript
When you press play, the transcript follows along with the recording. Clicking any line jumps straight to that moment, so you never have to scrub through an hour.

### 6 · Notes with timestamps
The notes are grouped into sections, and every point links back to where it was said. If the model cites a time that doesn't exist, I throw it away.

### 7 · Templates and action items
Changing the template rewrites the notes for that kind of meeting, like a sales call. Action items keep who said them and when, and you can assign each one to a teammate.

### 8 · Ask this meeting
You can also just ask the meeting a question. Here I'm asking what they decided about collecting more data. The answer comes back with timestamps, and each one takes you to the exact point in the call.

### 9 · Highlight and clip
The highlight button saves the last twenty seconds and the next ten, because by the time you think "that was important", the moment has already passed. You can also clip any range from the transcript, and share just that clip with someone who wasn't on the call.

### 10 · Opening the shared clip
Here's that link opened with no session at all. The clip plays with its own transcript, and nothing else from the meeting is exposed.

### 11 · Search and Ask across meetings
Search runs across every transcript and every set of notes, and results open right at the matching moment. Ask Sounding works across all your calls. Here I'm asking what was decided and who owns the next steps, and the answer links to each meeting and moment it came from.

### 12 · Trackers
Trackers watch for topics across every call. Keyword trackers look for exact words, and AI trackers look for meaning. So the deadline tracker picks up lines like "until early May to turn in their paper", even though nobody said the word deadline.

### 13 · Coaching
Coaching shows talk time, questions and long monologues for each person. Scorecards grade each call against the criteria set for that type of meeting.

### 14 · Playlists, companies and sharing
Playlists collect clips from different meetings, and play all runs them back to back, which is handy for onboarding. Companies are grouped automatically from attendee emails. And every meeting can be private, shared with a team or shared with the whole organization.

### 15 · Upload
The one thing I stubbed on purpose is the bot joining the call. A reliable meeting bot needs app reviews and a lot of work per platform, so I spent that time on what happens after the call instead. You upload the recording and it goes through exactly the same pipeline.

### 16 · Admin, while it processes
While that processes, this is the admin side. You can create teams and team leads, and set up meeting types that are detected automatically. There are also custom templates, scorecards and a dictionary for company words the transcript should always spell right.

### 17 · Upload finished
And it's done. About a minute from upload to titled, speaker-split notes.
